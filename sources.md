## Comparability warnings
- Swiggy's GOV includes delivery charges, packaging, user fees and taxes. Eternal's NOV is GOV minus all discounts. So 26.0% and 32.8% are on different bases, and the memo only uses them as a range.
- "Adjusted revenue minus contribution" (~18–23%) covers everything spent per order: delivery, platform-funded discounts, payments and support. It isn't delivery cost alone.
- Some press quotes Eternal's Rs 3,100 cr (Ind AS revenue) as revenue, or says EBITDA grew "155%". The real growth is 34% (606/451).
